## 1. The Core Malaysian Frameworks (DOSH & CIDB)

* 
* The Occupational Safety and Health (Amendment) Act 2022: This is the most crucial piece of regulatory text for your site. It officially came into force on June 1, 2024, repealing the old Factories and Machinery Act 1967 (FMA) and dramatically expanding safety laws to all Malaysian workplaces. It mandates hiring an OSH Coordinator for companies with five or more workers and significantly raises penalties for corporate non-compliance.
* Official PDF Link: You can download and link directly to the full gazetted text via the [UiTM Legal Repository Act 514 Amendment PDF](https://penang.uitm.edu.my/images/JKKPN/Penerbitan/Panduan/Versi_Penuh_AKKP_Pindaan_English.pdf). [1, 2] 
* Guidelines for Public Safety and Health at Construction Sites: Issued directly by the Department of Occupational Safety and Health (DOSH / JKKP), this rulebook lays down explicit guidelines for building operations, public protections, and strict structural engineering activities.
* Official PDF Link: Source the clean government copy via the [JPM Public Safety Construction Guidelines PDF](https://jpmihara.my/assets/files/gl_construction_site_bi.pdf). [3] 
* CIDB CIS 25:2018 (Construction Activities Risk Assessment - CARA): This standard details the mandatory hazard identification, risk assessment, and risk control (HIRARC) protocols required across all Malaysian engineering sites.
* Official Resource: Refer clients directly to the [CIDB Construction Industry Standard Portal](https://www.cidb.gov.my/eng/construction-industry-standard-cis/) to view updated compliance models. [4]
