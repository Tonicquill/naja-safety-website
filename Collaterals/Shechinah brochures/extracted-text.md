## Panel 3: Full Company Brochure Spread (IMG_20260904_153835.jpg)

**Top-left: OSHA Act Introduction**
> The Occupational Safety & Health Act 1994 (also known as OSHA) that is enforced by the Department of Occupational Safety & Health (also know as DOSH / JKKP) since 1994 has played a great role in reducing the accident rate and creating a safe & healthy working environment for all employers and employees in Malaysia. In order to expand and promote Occupational Safety & Health (OSH), we are dedicated to provide the best OSH services that suite the requirements of the law and industries. The key priority of our business is a commitment of continuous improvement to promote and encourage greater understanding, knowledge and awareness towards creating a safe and healthy working environment.

**About NAJA SAFETY SDN BHD**
> NAJA SAFETY was in cooperated in Malaysia since year 2005 and has been expanding from Johor, Melaka, Kuala Lumpur, Perak & Penang. We have served multinational companies throughout the region and have completed projects successfully with zero accident rates. We are currently based in Johor with our office situated in Taman Pelangi Indah, Ulu Tiram. We a proud to say that we are Safety Specialist in the field of building construction with a manpower network of more than 50 OSH practitioners.

**OUR VISION**
> To be the leading Occupational Safety and Health Services and training provider in Malaysia. To fulfil all Occupational Safety & Health requirements for industries and organizations using latest techniques, formats, guidelines and codes of practices.

**OUR MISSION**
> Assist the Government namely the Department of Occupational Safety & Health (DOSH / JKKP) on achieving their objective to reduce Occupational Accident rates in Malaysia. Provide quality Occupational Safety & Health services for industries and organizations to achieve better productivity through OSH management. Promote Occupational Safety & Health awareness among industries and organizations and creating an attitude of a safe working culture among workers.

**BENEFITS OF SAFETY**
> Promotion, education and awareness are vital and essential element of occupational safety and health programs in the ongoing effort to:
- Reduce and eliminate worker injuries or illness.
- Reduce accident costs.
- Increase productivity.
- Boost employee morale.
- Generate good will between employees and management.
- Lower operating costs.

**HOW SAFETY CAN SAFE COSTS?**
> The costs of accident prevention are relatively easy to assess in comparison with the costs of accidents. Firstly, there are the design costs, such as those costs required to install machine guards to protect workers. Secondly, there are the operational costs, such as the costs of running a safety department (e.g. training, protective clothing, etc.). Lastly, there are the planning and consequence-limiting costs which refer to "safeguarding the future", as for example in the costs of environmental sampling or the costs of safety audits.

**Our Services — INDUSTRIAL WASTE MANAGEMENT & ENVIRONMENT**
> The advancement of Malaysian Economy and Industries in various fields has possibilities to contribute more pollutants to the environment. There are stringent needs of control and treatment systems for various types of process and production activities as per statutory regulations. NAJA SAFETY has developed skills and expertise to advice organizations on suitable solutions to their environmental issues.

**The services include:**
- Disposal and recycling options.
- On-site waste clean up.
- Waste storage recommendations.
- Waste & Chemical labeling.
- Waste water treatment
- Air pollution Control
- Environmental Monitoring

**OSH MONITORING & ASSESSMENTS**
> Occupational Safety & Health has become a priority for many large organizations and industries. Government authorities namely the Department of Occupational Safety & Health (DOSH) is continuously enforcing and educating organizations the importance of OSH to reduce industrial accident rates and providing a safe & healthy workplace for all. NAJA SAFETY is dedicated to provide OSH related services to organizations. Our Monitoring and Assessment:
1. Chemical Exposure Monitoring.
2. Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) Inspection.
3. Noise mapping and Monitoring.
4. Chemical Health Risk Assessments (CHRA).
5. Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment & Control.
