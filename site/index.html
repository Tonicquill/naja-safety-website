20+ Years Keeping Malaysian Workplaces Safe

Under Section 29A of the newly enforced OSH (Amendment) Act 2022, any Malaysian enterprise employing 5 or more individuals is legally mandated to appoint a certified OSH Coordinator (OSH-C). Failing to secure this single baseline appointment exposes your organization, managing directors, and board owners to direct personal criminal prosecution, stop-work orders, and statutory fines of up to RM50,000.

RM0 Net Out-Of-Pocket Regulatory Training

Under HRD Corp Employers' Circular No. 7/2019, your corporate training levies do not sit safely indefinitely. They are subject to a strict, rolling 24-month forfeiture clause. Any accumulated balances left completely inactive past this 24-month window exceeding RM10,000 are automatically forfeited back to the central government pool. We audit your account status and design custom, 100% grant-permissible safety programs to deploy your funds productively before the rolling expiration window closes.
