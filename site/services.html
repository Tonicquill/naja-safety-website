Safety Services & Training Programmes

From OSH training and CIDB certification to full compliance consultancy — we are your one-stop safety partner.

A. OSH Training

Comprehensive Occupational Safety and Health programmes designed for Malaysian workplaces. Delivered on-site or in-classroom by experienced practitioners.

  • Incident & Accident InvestigationComprehensive training covering root-cause analysis, evidence gathering, and compliance reporting frameworks under NADOPOD Regulations 2004.Target Application: HSE Committee Members, Operational Managers, and HR Directors tasked with handling workplace accident response.
  • Safety & Health Committee TrainingStrategic implementation steps to establish, train, and run an effective, compliant internal safety council under the OSH (Safety and Health Committee) Regulations 1996.Target Application: Mandatory for all enterprises employing 40 or more workers, or operations handling high-risk chemical/mechanical manufacturing.
  • Chemical SafetyPractical handling procedures, personal protective equipment (PPE) deployment, and emergency spillage controls under USECHH Regulations 2000.Target Application: Factory operators, lab technicians, and warehouse logistics teams handling hazardous or scheduled toxic wastes.
  • Electrical SafetySpecialized risk awareness training highlighting high-voltage mitigation protocols, lockout-tagout (LOTO) isolation workflows, and shock prevention.Target Application: Plant maintenance technicians, facilities engineers, and mechanical repair crews working around live heavy machinery.
  • Emergency Response & Fire SafetyComprehensive crisis command structures, fire fighting equipment handling, and coordinated building evacuation drill management.Target Application: Appointed internal Emergency Response Team (ERT) captains, floor wardens, and security operations managers.
  • First Aid & CPRCertified life support instruction covering basic first aid, automated external defibrillator (AED) usage, choking management, and wound stabilization.Target Application: Mandatory for designated workplace first-aiders, factory supervisors, and project operations leads.
  • Forklift & Machinery SafetyRigorous operational competency training covering pre-shift equipment check sheets, payload center-of-gravity calculations, and safe rack maneuvering.Target Application: Material handlers, warehouse forklift drivers, and logistics fleet operators within manufacturing plants.
  • Advanced Warehouse Logistics & High-Racking Structural Auditing OperationsAdvanced competency training for warehouse operations managers covering high-racking load distribution analysis, pallet racking structural integrity audits, and aisle safety compliance under Malaysian Factory and Machinery Act 1967.Target Application: Warehouse operations directors, logistics fleet managers, and distribution center supervisors responsible for multi-tier storage facilities and high-volume inventory throughput.
  • HIRARC & Risk ManagementClear, actionable frameworks for mapping out Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Risk Control models to meet strict statutory inspection criteria.Target Application: Operations directors, safety executives, and project planners needing to eliminate structural workplace liabilities.
  • Safety Audit & Inspection TechniquesPractical methodology training for conducting regular internal site hazard audits, identifying compliance gaps, and constructing corrective action reports.Target Application: Internal safety auditors, operational supervisors, and plant managers preparing for formal government evaluations.
  • Ergonomics Risk Assessment (ERA) & Industrial Workstation Posture OptimizationScientific workstation analysis and musculoskeletal disorder (MSD) prevention training covering repetitive strain injury (RSI) mapping, anthropometric workstation redesign, and ergonomic compliance benchmarking.Target Application: Occupational health nurses, industrial hygienists, HR wellness coordinators, and factory line supervisors managing seated assembly or repetitive manual handling tasks.
  • Tailor-Made HSE ProgrammesCompletely customized safety modules engineered directly from your company's historic site incident data and industry risk matrices.Target Application: Heavy industrial operations, multi-national logistics setups, and unique operational plants looking for specific corporate risk reduction.

B. CIDB & Construction Safety Training

CIDB-certified instruction for construction sites. We cover everything from induction to scaffold safety — and we come to your site.

  • Safety Induction & AwarenessCore foundations of the construction safety landscape, site risk identification, and mandatory safety gear guidelines for civil projects.Target Application: General laborers, project engineers, and sub-contractor crews entering active development plots.
  • Working at Height (WAH) SafetySpecialized training focusing on fall arrest system configurations, full-body harness anchorage tracking, and safe mobile elevated work platform operations.Target Application: Roofing specialists, high-elevation painters, civil structural welders, and facade maintenance technicians.
  • Fall Protection Engineering & Technical Structural Anchor AuditingEngineering-grade instruction on fall arrest system design, permanent anchor point load testing, and structural connection verification for elevated construction and maintenance environments.Target Application: Structural engineers, rigging specialists, and construction safety officers overseeing vertical facade work, steel erection, and high-rise infrastructure projects.
  • Site Safety ManagementMaster compliance management structures tailored to coordinate multi-tier subcontractor arrays and ensure full CIDB structural compliance.Target Application: Main contractor project managers, site safety directors, and development consultants.
  • Toolbox Talk & Safety BriefingsPractical frameworks for delivering impactful daily pre-shift safety briefings that reinforce hazard awareness and site safety rules on the ground.Target Application: Site foremen, construction team leads, and safety supervisors managing daily shift turnarounds.
  • Green Card Related Training (SICW / CIDB Green Card)Official accredited safety induction program required under the CIDB Act to secure full, legal access to all construction zones across Malaysia.Target Application: Every individual operating on or visiting an active construction development—including site engineers, suppliers, and safety managers.
  • Scaffold Safety Awareness ProgramTechnical structural integrity training covering load limits, bracing setups, safe tie-back anchoring, and official inspection routines.Target Application: Scaffold assemblers, on-site safety inspectors, and structural development supervisors.

Note: We conduct CIDB Green Card training across Johor Bahru, Pasir Gudang, Sedenak, and nationwide.

C. Safety Consultancy & Support

Beyond training, we embed ourselves as your external compliance shield. Under the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (Act 514) and the OSH (Amendment) Act 2022, companies face mandatory compliance requirements. Non-compliance means heavy fines and stop-work orders.

  • Workplace Safety AssessmentsComplete structural facility safety evaluations designed to locate latent risk variables and establish reliable compliance baselines ahead of official audits.Target Application: Corporate boards, manufacturing executives, and business owners looking to verify their current legal protection standing.
  • Compliance & Risk AdvisoryClear guidance navigating the latest mandates of the OSH (Amendment) Act 2022, helping organizations avoid costly legal non-compliance penalties.Target Application: Small-to-medium enterprise (SME) directors transitioning their business practices to meet newly enforced safety thresholds.
  • Safety Documentation SupportProfessional preparation and maintenance of mandatory statutory records, including official machinery registers (PMA/PMT), SOP books, and safety policies.Target Application: Administrative managers and operations coordinators facing administrative backlogs ahead of compliance updates.
  • Supply of Competent Safety Supervisor & Environmental OfficerDirect field deployment of certified, registered safety practitioners (including Site Safety Supervisors and Environmental Officers) to manage day-to-day compliance.Target Application: Fast-moving civil projects, infrastructure developments, and manufacturing hubs requiring immediate, expert on-site supervision.
  • Industrial Waste Management & Environmental AdvisoryExpert guidance on disposal and recycling options, on-site waste clean-up, waste storage recommendations, waste & chemical labelling, wastewater treatment, air pollution control, and environmental monitoring aligned with DOE statutory requirements.Target Application: Manufacturing plants, chemical facilities, and industrial operations facing environmental compliance audits or expanding production capacity.
  • Supply of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)Direct sourcing and supply of certified head protection, face shields, eye protection, ear protection, respirators, fall arresters, safety shoes, and full-body harnesses matched to your site risk profile.Target Application: Site managers and procurement officers needing reliable, compliant PPE procurement for new projects or workforce expansion.
  • Safety Signages & First Aid EquipmentSupply of mandatory workplace safety signages, hazard warning labels, emergency exit markers, and stocked first aid boxes compliant with statutory OSH and Factory and Machinery Act requirements.Target Application: Facilities managers setting up new plants, renovating existing layouts, or replenishing expired first aid and fire safety consumables.
