## Reconstructed Educational Hook & Sales Copy (from historical description)

### The Cost-Prevention Pitch

The original "Benefits of Safety" section drew a sharp structural distinction between two cost categories:

#### Front-End Prevention Design Costs
- Machine guards
- Hazard controls
- Engineering-out of risks
- Proactive safety-system installation

#### Back-End Operational Liabilities (post-accident)
- Medical and compensation costs
- Regulatory fines and legal exposure
- Production downtime
- Reputational damage
- Morale degradation and staff turnover

### Core Narrative Angle
For managers and directors, the pitch was framed as a **baseline financial incentive**: investing in upfront safety design is exponentially cheaper than absorbing the downstream operational liabilities of an actual workplace accident. The text addressed promotion, education, and awareness as vital elements of occupational safety and health programmes.
