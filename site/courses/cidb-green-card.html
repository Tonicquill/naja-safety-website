CIDB Green Card Training (SICW)

Mandatory construction induction certification from CIDB Centre Code PLSICW20231022-068. Classroom and on-site delivery across Malaysia.

Book Green Card Training WhatsApp Naja Safety

What You Will Learn

The CIDB Green Card (SICW) programme covers the essential safety knowledge every construction worker must possess before entering a Malaysian building site. Our 1-day intensive ensures your workforce is compliant, protected, and productive.

  • Construction site hazard awareness and risk control
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) selection and use
  • Working at height basics and fall prevention
  • Safe material handling and housekeeping
  • Emergency response and first-aid awareness
  • CIDB statutory requirements and record-keeping
1 Day Classroom / On-site CIDB Certified

Course Syllabus / Modules

  1. Construction site hazard awareness � Identify common hazards on Malaysian construction sites and apply risk-control measures.
  2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) � Select, inspect, and correctly use helmets, gloves, boots, goggles, and hi-vis apparel.
  3. Working at height basics � Understand fall risks, harness use, and edge protection requirements.
  4. Safe material handling & housekeeping � Prevent musculoskeletal injuries and maintain tidy, safe site conditions.
  5. Emergency response & first aid � React to common site emergencies, use emergency equipment, and provide basic first-aid support.
  6. CIDB statutory requirements & record-keeping � Navigate CIDB Act 520 obligations and maintain accurate training records.
  7. Practical site induction demonstration � Apply classroom knowledge in a simulated site walkthrough and hazard-spotting exercise.

Learning Outcomes

  • Recognise and report common construction hazards before they cause incidents
  • Correctly wear, inspect, and maintain PPE for daily site operations
  • Follow safe procedures for working at height and material handling
  • Respond confidently to site emergencies and provide basic first aid
  • Demonstrate compliance with CIDB statutory requirements and record-keeping standards
  • Complete the practical site induction assessment successfully

Target Audience

Construction Workers

General labourers, tradespeople, and subcontractors who need mandatory induction certification to enter Malaysian construction sites.

Site Supervisors & Foremen

Frontline leaders responsible for enforcing safety standards and ensuring every team member holds valid CIDB Green Card credentials.

Contractors & Project Managers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CIDB Green Card and who needs it?
The CIDB Green Card (also known as SICW — Safety Induction for Construction Workers) is a mandatory construction induction certification required for all workers entering Malaysian construction sites. It proves the holder has undergone essential safety awareness training.
How long does the CIDB Green Card training take?
Naja Safety's CIDB Green Card training is typically completed in 1 day. We offer both classroom sessions at our Johor Bahru centre and on-site delivery for large contractor groups nationwide.
Is Naja Safety an authorised CIDB training centre?
Naja Safety operates under CIDB Centre Code PLSICW20231022-068. Our trainers are CIDB-certified instructors, and successful candidates receive official CIDB Green Card certification.
Can you conduct Green Card training on our construction site?
We regularly deliver on-site CIDB Green Card training for project teams across Johor Bahru, Pasir Gudang, Sedenak, and nationwide. Minimum class sizes and site safety requirements apply.
What should workers bring on training day?
Workers should bring a valid IC or passport, a recent passport-sized photograph, and wear comfortable clothing suitable for practical demonstrations. PPE will be provided during the session.
