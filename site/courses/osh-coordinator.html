The OSH-C Mandate

Any Malaysian enterprise employing 5 or more individuals is legally mandated to appoint an OSH Coordinator (OSH-C). This single baseline appointment is the first statutory shield between your organisation and direct criminal prosecution under the OSH Act.

  • Legally required for all workplaces with 5+ employees
  • Protects managing directors and board owners from personal liability
  • Required documentation: appointment letter, training records, competency certificate
  • Available on-site nationwide (Johor, KL, Penang, and beyond)

Frequently Asked Questions

Who must appoint an OSH Coordinator under Malaysian law?
Under Section 29A of the OSH (Amendment) Act 2022, any Malaysian employer with 5 or more workers must appoint an OSH Coordinator (OSH-C). This includes factories, construction sites, warehouses, offices, and retail operations.
What are the penalties for not appointing an OSH-C?
Failure to appoint an OSH-C exposes the organisation, managing directors, and board owners to direct personal criminal prosecution, stop-work orders, and statutory fines of up to RM500,000 under the OSH (Amendment) Act 2022.
How long does OSH Coordinator training take?
Naja Safety conducts flexible OSH-C development programmes typically ranging from 2 to 5 days, depending on class size and prior safety knowledge. On-site delivery is available nationwide across Johor and Peninsular Malaysia.
Can HRD Corp levy be used for OSH-C training?
Yes. OSH Coordinator training delivered by our HRD Corp-accredited trainer (ID 62976) may be claimable against your HRD Corp levy, subject to active balance thresholds and e-TRiS grant approvals.
What does the OSH-C training cover?
The programme covers OSH Act legal obligations, hazard identification, risk assessment, incident investigation, safety committee coordination, emergency response planning, and statutory reporting requirements — tailored to your industry and site risks.
