Do You Need CIDB Green Cards?

Under the CIDB Act 520, every person entering an active construction site must hold a valid CIDB Green Card (SICW). Site managers, main contractors, and developers face project delays and penalties if workers lack current certification. Many sites still rely on outdated or counterfeit cards.

The Stakes

Immediate Site Removal

CIDB enforcement officers conduct random site inspections. Workers without valid Green Cards can be removed immediately, halting your project.

Project Suspension Risk

Main contractors risk project suspension, and repeated violations can affect CIDB contractor grades — impacting future tenders.

Counterfeit Card Exposure

CIDB now scans QR codes for real-time verification. Cut-price counterfeit certificates from uncertified providers are instantly flagged.

Hazard Profile

Invalid or Expired Cards

Cards obtained through uncertified providers or past expiry date. CIDB now scans QR codes for real-time verification.

Untrained Subcontractors

Subcontractor crews often arrive on site without SICW certification, halting work until compliant.

Language Barriers

Foreign workers may not understand Malay or English safety briefings. We deliver training in multiple languages.

High Workforce Turnover

Construction sites with high turnover require continuous batch training to maintain 100% compliance.

Our Process

Step 01

Batch Registration

We collect worker details, passport/IC copies, and register the batch with CIDB e-Systems.

Step 02

Classroom or On-Site Induction

Official SICW curriculum delivered by CIDB-certified instructors. One-day intensive covering site hazards, PPE, emergency response, and legal obligations.

Step 03

Assessment & Certification

Workers sit the CIDB assessment. Successful candidates receive their Green Card with QR-verifiable serial number.

Step 04

Site Deployment Verification

We verify card validity with your site safety officer and provide a compliance attendance register for CIDB inspection.

Step 05

Renewal Tracking

Green Cards expire. We maintain a renewal calendar and notify you 60 days before expiry to prevent lapses.

Why Other Approaches Fail

Some providers offer cut-price "certificates" that CIDB does not recognise. Others conduct training in generic classrooms far from your site, causing worker downtime and transport costs. One-off training without renewal tracking leads to compliance gaps within months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Green Card valid?
CIDB Green Cards are typically valid for 3 years. Renewal requires re-assessment. We track expiry dates and remind you 60 days in advance.
Can foreign workers get a Green Card?
Yes. Foreign workers legally employed on Malaysian construction sites must hold a valid SICW. We process registration for both local and foreign workers.
Do supervisors and engineers need Green Cards too?
Yes. Every individual physically present on an active construction site must hold a valid SICW, including project managers, engineers, and visitors on extended stays.
What happens if CIDB inspects and finds uncertified workers?
Workers may be barred from site. Repeated violations can lead to project suspension and downgrade of your CIDB contractor registration. We provide same-day emergency batch training for inspection-triggered compliance gaps.
Can SICW training be claimed under HRD Corp?
Training is delivered by an HRD Corp Accredited Trainer (ID: 62976). Whether it can be claimed against your levy depends on your own HRD Corp registration, active balance, and programme alignment — we will confirm eligibility before you commit. We assist with e-TRiS documentation for batch training claims.
Can you train on our construction site?
Absolutely. We deliver SICW training directly on your site, minimising worker downtime and transport costs. All equipment and materials provided.
