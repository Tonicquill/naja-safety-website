About Naja Safety

Two decades of protecting people, places, and businesses across Malaysia.

Company Overview

Two decades of protecting people, places, and businesses across Malaysia.

Naja Safety Sdn Bhd (formerly known as Naja Safety) was incorporated in Malaysia in 2005 and has been expanding from Johor to Melaka, Kuala Lumpur, Perak, and Penang. We have served multinational companies throughout the region and have completed projects successfully with zero accident rates. We are currently based in Johor Bahru.

We are proud to say that we are safety specialists in the field of building construction, with a manpower network of 32 OSH practitioners supporting our training and consultancy operations nationwide.

Our Story

From a single consultant to a trusted nationwide safety partner.

2006
James Issachar joins the field as an OSH Consultant, building hands-on experience across manufacturing floors and construction sites.
2015
James becomes Director of Naja Safety Sdn Bhd, formalising the consultancy into a full-service safety training and compliance firm.
2017
Sister company Naja Scaffolding Sdn Bhd is incorporated (Reg: 201701012443 / 1226608-V), extending our construction safety reach with scaffolding supply and expertise.
2018
James founds Shechinah Association Johor Bahru, a charity dedicated to community welfare — reflecting a belief that safety extends beyond the workplace into society.
Today
Naja Safety serves 158+ companies nationwide with a manpower network of 32 OSH practitioners. We have completed projects successfully with zero accident rates, from Econpile and Penta Ocean Malaysia to GP Batteries and CIDB.

James Issachar A/L Daniel

Director & Lead Trainer

James Issachar did not start in a boardroom. He started at NIOSH as a vocational trainee. Through discipline, field experience, and a genuine commitment to protecting workers, he built Naja Safety into one of Johor Bahru's most trusted safety consultancies.

With 20+ years in manufacturing, construction, and safety consultancy, James holds:

  • HRD Corp Certified Trainer (ID: 62976)
  • CIDB Certified Instructor
  • OSH Consultant since May 2006
  • Director, Naja Safety Sdn Bhd since January 2015

Beyond business, James serves as Chairman of Shechinah Association Johor Bahru, a charity he founded in 2018 to give back to the community that shaped him.

Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be the leading Occupational Safety and Health Services and training provider in Malaysia. To fulfil all Occupational Safety & Health requirements for industries and organizations using latest techniques, formats, guidelines and codes of practices.

Our Mission

Assist the Government namely the Department of Occupational Safety & Health (DOSH / JKKP) on achieving their objective to reduce Occupational Accident rates in Malaysia. Provide quality Occupational Safety & Health services for industries and organizations to achieve better productivity through OSH management. Promote Occupational Safety & Health awareness among industries and organizations and creating an attitude of a safe working culture among workers.

Protection First

Every worker deserves to go home safe. We design training and systems that prevent harm before it happens.

Partnership, Not Transaction

We build long-term relationships. Our clients return because we stay with them through audits, incidents, and growth.

Real-World Learning

Theory alone does not save lives. Our training is grounded in actual site experience — Malaysian factories, construction sites, and industrial plants.

Community Commitment

Through Shechinah Association Johor Bahru, we extend our care beyond the workplace into the communities we serve.

Community Involvement

Giving back is part of who we are.

Shechinah Association Johor Bahru

Founded in 2018 by James Issachar, Shechinah Association Johor Bahru is a charity committed to community welfare in Johor. James serves as Chairman, channeling the same discipline and care from his safety career into social impact.
