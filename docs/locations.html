Where We Deliver

Our lead trainer and freelance network cover seven states across Peninsular Malaysia. Travel costs are built into on-site quotations — no hidden surcharges.

Johor

Primary base: Johor Bahru (Mount Austin). Regular coverage of Tebrau, Pasir Gudang, Senai, Pengerang, Iskandar Puteri, and Skudai. Same-day site visits available.

  • OSH Coordinator (OSH-C) training
  • CIDB Green Card (SICW) on-site
  • Chemical Safety & CHRA
  • Scaffold & Working at Height

Selangor & Kuala Lumpur

Monthly scheduled runs to Shah Alam, Petaling Jaya, Subang, Klang, and Kuala Lumpur city centre. Ideal for central-region companies with multiple sites.

  • HIRARC & Risk Management
  • Safety Audit & Inspection
  • First Aid & CPR certification
  • Forklift & MEWP training

Melaka

Bi-monthly training blocks in Ayer Keroh and Melaka city. Popular with manufacturing clients in the industrial zones.

  • Fire Safety & Emergency Response
  • Chemical Handling & Spill Control
  • Noise Monitoring & LEV Inspection

Perak

On-demand deployment to Ipoh, Batu Gajah, and Kampar. Suitable for mining, plantation, and manufacturing sites.

  • OSH Monitoring & Assessments
  • Environmental Officer Training
  • Waste Management Compliance

Penang

Quarterly runs to George Town and Bayan Lepas. Strong demand from electronics and semiconductor manufacturing clusters.

  • Chemical Health Risk Assessment (CHRA)
  • Air Quality Sampling
  • Industrial Hygiene Consultancy

Negeri Sembilan

Scheduled visits to Seremban, Port Dickson, and Nilai. Serving oil & gas support services and construction contractors.

  • Working at Height (WAH)
  • Demolition Safety
  • Temporary Works Coordination
